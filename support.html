REPORT AN ISSUE

What to send us.

The more we know up front, the faster we can fix it. A short email with these details is perfect.

  • Who and which appYour name, your company and the product or app that has the problem.
  • What happenedWhat you saw, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • Steps to repeat itWhat you clicked or did just before it went wrong.
  • Where it happenedLive or test version, plus the browser or phone you used.
  • ScreenshotsA screenshot or short screen recording helps a lot.
  • How many peopleOne user, some users, or everyone? This sets the priority.

APPS WE DIDN’T BUILD

Taking over from another team?

We can look after apps built by someone else. We start with a code review, so you know what you have before we take it on.

  1. 01

    Review

    We review the code, hosting and open issues, and tell you honestly what we find.

  2. 02

    Hand over

    We get access to code, servers and accounts, and write down how everything fits together.

  3. 03

    Set up

    Monitoring, alerts and backups, plus the response targets we agree with you.

  4. 04

    Look after

    Monthly updates, fixes and a short report on what we did.

FAQ

Support questions.

Found a security problem in an app we built, or on this site? Email security@devsynth.us create inbox.

Can you support an app another team built?

Yes. We start with a code review so you know what you have, then take it on if it makes sense for both sides.

Who will work on my app?

Engineers who know your product. On the Priority plan you get a named engineer.

How do you report what you did?

You get a short monthly report: what we updated, what we fixed and what we recommend next.

What does a plan cost?

It depends on the size of your product and the level of care, so we quote after a short call. We don’t publish fixed prices.
