The short version

  • We collect what you send us (name, email, project details) and basic site usage data.
  • We use it to reply to you, run the site and improve our marketing.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We use a few named tools, such as Google Analytics, LinkedIn and Calendly.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

What this policy covers

This policy covers devsynth.us, our contact form, call bookings, emails and marketing. Data we handle for clients inside the products we build is covered by our contracts with those clients, not by this policy.

What we collect

What you give us: your name, work email, company, what you need and any project details you send through our form, by email or when you book a call. If you apply for a job, your CV and portfolio.

What we collect automatically: your IP address, device and browser type, pages you visit, the page that sent you to us, campaign tags in the link (UTM parameters) and cookies. See Cookies and tracking.

What we get from others: details you share with us on LinkedIn, for example through a LinkedIn lead form, and referrals from people who know you. Ali: confirm the full list of sources

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and prepare a proposal.
  • To schedule and hold calls.
  • To run, secure and improve the website.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns work (analytics).
  • To send occasional updates, only if you agreed to them.
  • To review job applications.
  • To meet legal duties.

If you’re in the EU or UK, we rely on your consent, on steps you asked us to take before a contract, on our legitimate interest in running and growing our business, or on a legal duty, depending on the purpose. lawyer: map each purpose to a legal basis

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the business, such as hosting, email, scheduling, analytics and our CRM.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if Devsynth is part of a merger or sale.

Your data is stored with providers in the United States and may be accessed by our team in Pakistan to answer you and deliver our services. We protect it with contracts, access controls and limited access. lawyer: transfer mechanism for EU/UK data

How long we keep it

  • Messages from people who don’t become clients: X months.
  • Client contacts: for as long as we work together, plus X years.
  • Job applications: X months.
  • Analytics data: as set in each tool for example 14 months in GA4.

How we protect it

We use encrypted connections, limit access to the people who need it and protect our accounts with two-step sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time with the link in each email.

EU and UK: you also have the right to object, to limit how we use your data, to move it and to complain to your local data protection authority.
