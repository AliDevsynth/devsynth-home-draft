OUR STORY

Built by engineers who ship.

Founders and product teams in the US, Canada, the UAE and Europe, from first MVPs to platforms with real users and real payments.

Devsynth was started by engineers who had spent years building and running products at scale, and kept seeing the same thing go wrong with outside teams: developers who went quiet, builds left half finished, and code nobody wanted to own.

So we built the team we would want to hire. Senior engineers who show working software every week, work inside your tools, and take responsibility for what they ship.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we often get.

Who owns the code and designs?
You do, from the first commit. Everything is kept in your repositories and accounts, and you get a documented handover whenever you want one.
Where is your team?
Our engineers and designers work from Karachi (Pakistan), the UAE and Saskatoon (Canada), and we have a US office in St. Petersburg, Florida.
Do you work in our time zone?
Our working day overlaps with US and Canadian business hours, so we join your standups and answer questions the same day.
Can we start small?
Yes. New products start with a 1 to 2 week discovery, and teams can start with a paid 2-week trial sprint before committing to more.
