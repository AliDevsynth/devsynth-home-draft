FAQ

Ask us anything.

Tap a question to see the answer. These are the ones founders and product teams ask us most.

How do we get started?
Book a 30-minute call. We’ll look at your idea and where your product is today, then recommend the right way to work together. New products start with a 1 to 2 week discovery; teams that need engineers start with a paid 2-week trial sprint.
Fixed price or a monthly team: which one is right for me?
Fixed price suits a new product or a feature with a clear goal: we scope it, agree the fee and deliver in milestones. A monthly team suits a product you already run and want to ship faster, without hiring first. Not sure? We’ll tell you honestly on the first call.
How much will my project cost?
It depends on the scope, so we don’t publish fixed prices. After the first call (and discovery, for a new product) you get a clear scope and a quote before any work starts.
How will I know how the work is going?
You get a weekly demo of working software and one person to go to with questions. On a monthly team, our engineers join your standups, your repo and your sprint board.
Can you work with my existing team and codebase?
Yes. We can add 1 to 3+ engineers and a PM to your team, working in your tools and your repo. Many teams start with the 2-week trial sprint to see how it fits.
Do you build AI features?
Yes: AI agents, RAG assistants that answer from your own data, and workflow automation, built on models like Claude and OpenAI. We’ll also tell you when AI isn’t the right tool for the job.
Who owns the code?
You do. The code lives in your repository, and it is yours from the first commit.
What happens after launch?
We can stay on for support, fixes and new features, or hand everything over to your team with documentation. You can scale the team up or down month to month.
