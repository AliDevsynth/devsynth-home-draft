FAQ

Questions about MVPs.

Have another one? Ask us on a call.

How small should an MVP be?

Small enough to launch soon, big enough to prove the one thing that matters most. Discovery is where we agree that line together.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. Discovery takes 1 to 2 weeks, and you get a timeline with milestones before the build starts.

Can you work without a technical co-founder?

Yes. Many founders come to us without one. We explain decisions in plain words and you own all the code.

Do I own the code?

Yes. It lives in your repository from the first commit.

What happens after launch?

We can stay on for support, fixes and new features, or hand everything over to your team with documentation.

What drives the cost?

Mostly scope, the number of platforms (web, iOS, Android), integrations like payments, and how much design work is needed. You get a fixed quote after discovery.
