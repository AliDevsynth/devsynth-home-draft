OUR STORY

Built by engineers who ship.

Devsynth was started by engineers who had spent years building and running products at scale, and kept seeing the same thing go wrong with outside teams: developers who went quiet, builds that were half finished, and code nobody wanted to own.

So we built the team we would want to hire. Senior engineers who show working software every week, work inside your tools, and take responsibility for what they ship. Today we build for founders and product teams in the US, Canada, the UAE and Europe, from first MVPs to platforms with real users and real payments.

HOW WE WORK

What you can count on.

Six practices you can check on every project, whether it’s a two-week sprint or a two-year partnership.

A demo every week

You see working software every week, not a status report. If something slips, you hear it from us first.

One person you can always reach

You get one point of contact who knows your product, and the engineers who build it join the calls.

Your repo and your tools from day one

We work in your repository, your tracker and your sprint board, and follow your code review rules.

Same-day answers in US hours

Our working day overlaps with US and Canadian hours, so questions don’t wait overnight.

You own the code from the first commit

Everything lives in your accounts. When you want to take it in-house, the handover is documented and clean.

We tell you when not to build

If you don’t need AI, a bigger team or us at all, we will say so on the first call.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we often get.

More answers on the home page FAQ, or ask us directly on a call.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do, from the first commit. Everything is kept in your repositories and accounts, and you get a documented handover whenever you want one.

Do you work in our time zone?

Our working day overlaps with US and Canadian business hours, so we join your standups and answer questions the same day.

Can we start small?

Yes. New products start with a 1 to 2 week discovery, and teams can start with a paid 2-week trial sprint before committing to more.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, we can sign your NDA before the first detailed call.
