FAQ

Questions about AI features.

Have another one? Ask us on a call.

What if AI isn’t the right fit?

Then we’ll tell you during discovery, before you pay for a build. Sometimes a simpler rule, search or automation does the job better.

How do you handle wrong or made-up answers?

We answer from your own data, test the feature on real questions before launch and send hard questions to a person. After launch we keep checking quality.

Which model do you use? Can we switch later?

We use models like Claude and OpenAI and pick the one that fits your use case. We build so the model can be changed later without a rebuild.

Is our data safe?

We agree up front which data the AI can use and where it goes, and we build around your security rules. Ali: confirm the exact data-handling promise

Who owns the code and prompts?

You do. Everything lives in your repository from the first commit.

What drives the cost?

Mostly the use case, the state of your data, how many systems the AI has to connect to and how much testing it needs. You get a quote after discovery.
