FAQ

Questions about our services.

Have another one? Ask us on a call.

Which service do I need?

You don’t have to know. Tell us what you’re building on a call, and we’ll recommend the services and the way of working that fit. If something isn’t worth building yet, we’ll say so.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The code lives in your repository and it is yours from the first commit.

Can you work with my existing team?

Yes. Our engineers can join your standups, your repo and your sprint board on a monthly plan. Many teams start with a paid 2-week trial sprint.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope, so we don’t publish fixed prices. After the first call (and discovery, for a new product) you get a clear scope and a quote before any work starts.
